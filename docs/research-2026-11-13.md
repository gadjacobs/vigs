# Do the other markets (handicaps, correct score, first-half result, combos) offer a way in? (13 Nov 2026)

Data: 47,698 settled matches, 16 Oct-13 Nov 2026, each with its last prematch
snapshot: main-market odds, SportyBet's own probabilities for them, and the
`extra` prices (first-half 1X2 / double chance / handicap, handicap 0:1 to 3:0,
correct score, result & both teams score, exact team goals, odd/even).
Errors clustered by league and round; first two thirds of days against the last third.

## 2. Is any outcome priced out of line with SportyBet's own probabilities?

Some extra outcomes have a true chance that follows exactly from the published
main-market probabilities: 0:0 = under 0.5; home scores exactly k = the home
team-goals lines; draw with no goals = under 0.5; draw with goals = draw − under 0.5.
Price × that chance, over all 47,698 matches:

| Outcome | Median | 1st-99th percentile | Ever above 1.00 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exact home / away goals 0, 1, 2, 3+ | 0.926 | 0.924-0.928 | never |
| Draw & both score, yes / no | 0.926 | 0.925-0.927 | never |
| Correct score 0:0 | 0.909 | 0.909-0.910 | never |

So each market is one fixed cut applied to the game's true probability, from one
engine: 7.4% on exact goals and result-and-both-score, 9.1% on correct score.
There is no outcome, team, score or price where a model of the true chance could
find a price above it, because the price is that chance times a constant below 1.
Together with the earlier findings (main markets calibrated to ±0.4%, in play
too; nothing by round, league, hour, market or game state) this closes the
question for vFootball: the result is a random draw at known probabilities and
every price sits a fixed margin under them.

## What follows

- No model can raise the long-run return above the cheapest legs' 97.6-98.4%.
- The extra markets add no cheaper legs. Their short-priced handicaps tie the
  main markets; everything else costs two to ten times as much per ₦1.
- What remains is cost and variance, which the app already steers: legs at
  1.20-1.25 with the multi-bet bonus, straight slips, one bold slip for a goal.
