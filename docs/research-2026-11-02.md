# Are live prices right for each game state? And what does a leg really cost? (2 Nov 2026)

Data: `live/` (one row per match in play, every ~15 s, 10 Oct-2 Nov) joined to
results; prematch `odds/` snapshots of 31 Oct and 1 Nov. Everything is read
against SportyBet's own probability.

## 1. Live calibration by game state (the test `docs/feed-schema.md` left open)

2,740,480 outcome readings (one per match per 5-minute bucket) on 39,767 matches,
1X2 and every goals line. Cells: market kind x minute bucket (0-14 ... 75-89) x
probability band (<10, 10-30, 30-50, 50-70, 70-90, 90+%), 141 with at least 300
readings in both periods. Discovery on the first two thirds of days, confirmation on
the last third, errors clustered by league and round, BH at 10% across all cells.

| Cells | Pass on discovery | Confirmed later | Same sign later |
|---|---|---|---|
| 141 | 0 | 0 | 44.7% |

Nothing: SportyBet's live probability is as well calibrated as its prematch one in
every minute and score state we can slice (rate within 0.1% of the probability in
every band). There is no live edge to select on.

## 2. What a leg costs depends on its chance, not on the market

Realized payback per ₦1 over all live readings, by SportyBet's probability:

| Chance | <10% | 10-30% | 30-50% | 50-70% | 70-90% | 90%+ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pays back | 71.5% | 92.3% | 96.0% | 96.7% | 97.7% | 98.3% |

Prematch (162,124 outcomes, first reading of each match), odds x SportyBet's
probability:

| Odds | ≤1.10 | 1.10-1.20 | 1.20-1.25 | 1.25-1.30 | 1.30-1.40 | 1.40-1.50 | 1.50-1.70 | 1.70-2 | 2-3 | 3-5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pays back | 98.4% | 97.8% | 97.6% | 97.4% | 97.1% | 96.9% | 96.5% | 96.2% | 95.4% | 93.4% |

The same at every market kind (over, under, team goals, double chance: 97.1-97.6%
at 1.20-1.30). So a slip's cost is set by its legs' odds, and legs at 1.20-1.25
(just inside the multi-bet bonus's 1.20 floor) are the cheapest that qualify.

## 3. The bonus estimate was too high

`lib/money.ts` and the Codes card stood in a flat 4% cut for SportyBet's chances
(a leg pays back 96.15%). Measured, a 1.20-1.25 leg pays back 97.6%, and the bonus
ratio grows with how far the legs' payback sits under 1 (ratio = payback^(1-n) - 1,
inside the range for n legs). An 11-leg slip at 7.94 odds: +35% by the old stand-in,
+27% by the measured curve (`lib/bonus.ts` `legPayback`), +25% paid by SportyBet
(slip WP7AHU, ₦2,030 → ₦20,146.66). Exact needs SportyBet's own probability per
leg; the curve gets within 2-3 points.

## What this means for selection

Accuracy: nothing to select on, in play or before. Where the app can still help is
cost: shorter odds per leg, no leg below the bonus floor, no Flex above about 10
legs, and showing the payout SportyBet will actually pay.
