# What predicts vFootball beyond the price? (31 Oct 2026)

Data: 36,961 matches with SportyBet prices (9-31 Oct), 1.5 million priced
outcomes (51 markets), every snapshot's main prices, SportyBet's own
probabilities, and extras (correct score, handicaps, result + both teams to
score, odd/even). Baseline: SportyBet's published probability for each outcome.
Every test judges on days the method never saw.

## Results

| Idea | What it says | Held out |
|---|---|---|
| SportyBet's probability | Calibrated to within ±0.4% in every chance band over 1.5M outcomes | the bar |
| Gradient-boosted model on everything below (form, correct-score prices, price moves, league rounds, hour, margins) | Early stopping kept 1 tree: nothing generalises past training days | no gain (+0.04e-4 log loss/row) |
| Correct-score prices vs the market's own price | Where they disagree, results follow neither more than chance | ±0.6%, noise |
| Price moves before kickoff | vFootball prices never move between first sight and kickoff | none to use |
| Team form (last 5 / 20 goals against price) | Hot or cold sides land within ~2% of price, mixed sign | noise |
| Replays of the same fixture over a year | Price already tracks the replay rate (r = 0.995) | worse than price |
| Odds × SportyBet probability | No outcome pays more than SportyBet's own chance (0 of 1.5M) | — |
| League round ran hot (goals ≥ +0.2 a match over its prices) | Overs in the next round looked +1.2% (z 3.7 a half) | **did not hold** once errors were clustered by round (below) |

## Correction, 31 Oct evening: errors clustered by round

Legs in the same round rise and fall together (a quiet round sinks every over
in it), so treating them as independent overstated the z-scores above. Redone
on 38,395 matches with errors clustered by round, discovering on the first two
thirds of days and confirming on the last third, with BH at 10% across all tests:

| Family | Tests | Pass on discovery | Confirmed later | Same sign later |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Last round's goals / home, away, first-half goals / home win, draw, away win / both score (same league 1 and 2 rounds back, other leagues just finished) → next round's 14 markets | 672 | 1 | 0 | 45% |
| Market × chance level (the "lean") | 119 | 0 | 0 | 51% |
| League × market × chance level | 468 | 0 | 0 | 47% |
| Hour of day × market × chance level | 669 | 0 | 0 | 46% |

Nothing holds; agreement between the two periods is a coin toss. The hot-round
idea (+1.7 z on discovery, +0.1 on confirmation for overs 2.5) is switched off
in the app (lib/hotflag.ts); its overs slips are still logged with no stake so
Record keeps measuring it. The first 27 live ones: 0 landed, legs 153 won
against 170.9 priced. The "lean" (markets above their price at a chance level,
used by Steady, Tight games and leg choice) is no better than chance either.
